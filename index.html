☀️ How solar works

Solar panels contain photovoltaic (PV) cells, usually silicon. Sunlight knocks electrons loose, creating DC current. An inverter converts it to AC for your home. Surplus can feed the grid (net metering) or charge batteries.

🔌 Key terms

  • kW – power (rate). A 3 kW system produces up to 3 kW in full sun.
  • kWh / unit – energy used over time. 1 kW for 1 hour.
  • Peak sun-hours – hours of 1 kW/m² equivalent sun per day (India ≈ 4.5–6).
  • Performance ratio – real-world losses (~75–85% efficiency).

🧱 Panel types

  • Mono PERC / TOPCon – 20–23% efficient, best for limited roofs.
  • Polycrystalline – cheaper, 16–18%, being phased out.
  • Thin-film – flexible, low efficiency, niche use.

System types

On-grid

No battery; cheapest, uses net metering. Shuts down during grid outage for safety.

Off-grid

Battery-based, fully independent. Costlier; good for remote areas.

Hybrid

Grid + battery. Gives backup in cuts and savings in daytime.

Frequently asked questions

How long do solar panels last?

Typically 25–30 years, with output dropping about 0.5% per year. Inverters usually need replacing in 8–12 years.

Will solar work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes, at reduced output (roughly 10–50% of clear-sky). Annual estimates already include this.

How much maintenance is needed?

Clean panels with water every 2–4 weeks (more in dusty areas), early morning or evening. Check for shading and loose wiring yearly.

How much roof space do I need?

About 8–10 m² per kW. A typical 3 kW home system needs 25–30 m².

What is net metering?

Your meter records units exported to the grid and credits them against units imported, lowering your bill. Rules and rates differ by state — check your DISCOM.

Is solar really good for the environment?

Yes. Each kWh from solar avoids about 0.8 kg of CO₂ from India's coal-heavy grid, and a panel repays its manufacturing energy in 1–3 years.

Which direction should panels face?

In India (northern hemisphere), south-facing at a tilt close to your latitude (15–25° for most cities).
