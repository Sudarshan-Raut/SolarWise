# SolarWise ☀️

**Intelligent Renewable Energy & Solar System Platform**

SolarWise is a web platform that helps households move to solar power. It turns complex solar data into clear, actionable insights.

🔗 **Live demo:** https://sudarshan-raut.github.io/SolarWise/

## Features
1. **Solar Sizing Calculator**: required kW from your bill, tariff and roof area
2. **Household Energy Analyzer**: ranks appliances by consumption
3. **Solar Potential Calculator**: suitability score from orientation, tilt and shading
4. **Weather & Solar Generation**: daily and seasonal output estimates
5. **ROI & Payback Calculator**: net investment, subsidy, payback period, 25-year savings
6. **Solar + Battery Calculator**: battery and inverter sizing for power cuts
7. **Dashboard**: all results in interactive Chart.js charts
8. **Solar Knowledge Centre**: how solar works, panel types, FAQs

## Tech Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js

## Future Scope
IoT smart-meter integration, net-metering projections, mobile app, community solar tools.
